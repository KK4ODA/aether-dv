**Radio tests in one click.** Diagnostics has a new **Radio test** card for recording a test and sending the results:

1. Type what you're testing and click **Start test**. Every port records its data, CI-V traffic, timing and route state.
2. While testing, click a marker when you do something by hand: **Keyed voice**, **Voice ended**, **Changed a radio setting**, **Something went wrong**. You can also type your own.
3. Click **Finish test**, then **Copy summary** and paste it to the developer. If the full recording is wanted, **Upload for the developer** opens the upload page and **Show file** finds the file.

The summary lists your markers and each port's state. For every transmission it shows how long the radio took to key up and its real time on air. It also shows time spent waiting and why, plus any warnings. On radios with CI-V (the IC-9700), the recording includes the CI-V traffic and the radio's own transmit/receive state.

Also:
- On Icom-controller repeaters, the repeater access check now explains that no acknowledgement is normal. Those repeaters only reply when they refuse to relay.
- From a terminal: `aetherdv-cli test start`, `test mark`, `test finish`.

The per-port capture buttons in Diagnostics are replaced by Radio test. `capture = true` in the config still records everything from start-up.

Still **tested in simulation only**: no real radios, repeaters or D-RATS stations yet.
