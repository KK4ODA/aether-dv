**Port plates that look clickable, and a route check before sending through a repeater.**

- **The port plates in the top bar show they open.** A plate with route and channel details now has a chevron at its right edge that brightens when you point at it, and a tooltip. Click it for the radio's call signs, the route and the channel.
- **On a repeater port, Aether DV checks the radio before sending.** With CI-V Transceive off, the radio doesn't announce changes, and Aether DV only re-read its call signs once a minute. Now, before the first transmission after 20 seconds of quiet, it reads UR, R1, R2 and duplex again. If they changed (for example R2 set to the gateway), the message waits and the panel explains why. The overs of one file transfer don't wait for this. Simplex ports are unchanged.

Still **tested on the bench only**: two radios on simplex, no repeaters or D-RATS stations yet. Test 3 confirmed the IC-9700's call signs are read correctly over CI-V in VFO, memory and DR mode.
