**Repeater operation.** Aether DV now knows where your data goes on a D-STAR repeater and takes turns with voice users.

- **Choose who receives your data, per radio port:** this repeater only (the default), or everyone on the reflector it's linked to. Set it in the config file's `[port.route]` section. The example configs show how.
- **Checks the radio before sending.** UR, R1 and R2 must match the choice: R2 blank keeps data on the repeater, R2 = the gateway sends it to the reflector.
  - On radios with CI-V (the IC-9700, and others where the radio answers), Aether DV reads them itself.
  - Otherwise it asks you to confirm them each session, after a reconnect, and after 30 minutes without sending.
- **Never re-sends a gateway command.** A link or unlink command left in UR is blocked when R2 is the gateway.
- **Reflector scope is deliberate.** You confirm it once per session and again when the link changes. Files there are limited to 8 KiB, and broadcast pings aren't answered.
- **Takes turns with voice.** Where CI-V reports the channel, Aether DV waits for voice to end plus a 10-second gap. It also:
  - keeps each transmission short and leaves gaps between them so others can break in;
  - keeps to an air-time budget: 25 % on a repeater, 10 % on a reflector;
  - pauses transfer timers while someone else holds the channel.
- **Pause sending** from the port panel, or for 30 minutes during a net.
- **Port panel.** Click a port in the top bar to see the radio's call signs next to what's needed, and why the port is waiting. It also has a repeater access check, which reports the repeater's acknowledgement, BER and linked reflector.
- **Radio Setup:** each radio has a new "Using a repeater" section.

Without CI-V, voice users are only noticed when their radio sends position data. Pause sending during voice contacts and nets.

Still **tested in simulation only**: a simulated IC-9700 and a simulated repeater. Nothing has been tested through a real repeater or with real D-RATS stations yet, and the timing values will change after on-air tests.
