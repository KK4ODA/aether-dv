**From bench Test 5 (IC-9700 and ID-52 PLUS).** Full results: `docs/bench-results.md`.

- **Radio flow control, on by default.** Both radios send "wait" (XOFF) and "go on" (XON) when a larger block fills their buffer. Aether DV now sends in small pieces, pauses on XOFF and resumes on XON. The two bytes no longer count as received data. Without this, full-size file transfers could overflow the radio.
  - **Action needed:** if your configuration file has `flow = "none"` (the old examples and bench guide did), change it to `flow = "xonxoff"` or delete the line.
- **Data from other stations no longer counts as voice.** The IC-9700 reports another station's data as voice. On a repeater, Aether DV would then have waited 10 seconds after every data transmission, including each acknowledgement. Transmissions that carry D-RATS data now get only the short pause.
- **Measured air time:** 0.67 s + 1 s per 122 bytes. Estimates and the radio test summary use it. The summary compares real air time with the on-air estimate, without key-up.
- **Radio test summary:** a new **write s** column shows when the PC finished writing each transmission. That separates a slow PC or USB link from the radio's own key-up delay. Flow-control events appear in the timeline.

Still **tested on the bench only**: two radios on simplex, no repeaters or D-RATS stations yet.
